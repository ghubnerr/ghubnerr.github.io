The AI Guild @ FIU

Since 2023, I’ve been teaching semi-weekly workshops on machine learning for students at FIU. We like to re-implement and learn from key research papers while building strong fundamentals in the field. A few of my favorite lectures have been on multimodality, Variational Auto-Encoders, and Reinforcement Learning.
